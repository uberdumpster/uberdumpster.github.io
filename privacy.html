2. Advertising and your choices

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads). Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain U.S. states are shown a consent / privacy message provided by Google. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → AD PRIVACY SETTINGS in the Game (shown in regions where it applies). If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Purchasing "Remove Ads" removes banner and interstitial ads.

See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. How long we keep information

  • Account, progress, friends and drawn icons: until you delete your account.
  • Ranking entries: until you delete your account or ask us to delete them.
  • Support messages and reports: up to 1 year after they are resolved, unless a longer period is needed to handle a dispute or required by law.
  • Advertising data is kept by Google according to Google's own policies.

5. Your rights and deleting your data

You can delete your account in the Game from Settings → Delete Account. This deletes your game account and the data saved with it. To also remove your ranking entry or support messages, or to access or correct your information, e-mail uberdumpster@gmail.com with your player ID (shown in the Game). Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing and to complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 16 (or a higher age where required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from such children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with the providers above, which use industry-standard security. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for important changes, notify you in the Game.
